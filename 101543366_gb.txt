## Information about George Brown College

The college started in 1967 as part of Ontario’s community college system.
It’s named after George Brown, who was a newspaper publisher and a politician.

School colours are blue and white, and their sports teams are called the Huskies

George Brown has three campuses in downtown Toronto
1. Casa Loma Campus
2. St. James Campus
3. Waterfront Campus(which has a new building "Limberlost Place)
