George Brown College (Toronto) is one of Canada’s top institutions for applied learning and career-focused education. 
It offers programs in technology, business, design, health sciences, and community services, all developed in collaboration with industry partners. 
The college’s downtown campuses provide students with access to real-world learning, modern facilities, and placement opportunities. 
Strong ties to local employers ensure that graduates are ready to meet industry demands and contribute to the Canadian workforce.
Added information about George Brown College’s partnerships and co-op programs.
George Brown College operates St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront campuses.
