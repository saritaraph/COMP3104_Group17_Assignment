Information about George Brown College

George Brown College was chartered by the government of Ontario in 1966, and opened the following year.

As of now, it consists of three different campuses, that collectively offer over 170 different full-time programs.
The three campuses are:
- Casa Loma Campus (the one that our program is a part of)
- St. James Campus
- Waterfront Campus
