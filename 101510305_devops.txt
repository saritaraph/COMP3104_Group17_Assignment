Information about the COMP3104 DevOps course

The COMP 3104 DevOps course is offered at George Brown College, as a part of the Computer Programming & Analysis program.

The program is offered both online, and hybridly (a mix of online and in-person classes). If you choose the in-person program, classes are held at GBC's Casa Loma Campus.

The course itself focuses on teaching students the procedures, protocols, and best practices of a DevOps engineer. It aims to teach students the importance of DevOps when it comes to software development, by guiding them on what skills and knowledge they can bring to increase productivity.

It makes use of hands-on examples and exercises so that students can emulate working in a proper workplace environment, using industry standard tools and software.
