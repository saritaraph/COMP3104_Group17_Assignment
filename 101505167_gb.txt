About George Brown College (GBC):

George Brown College (GBC) is a leading institution 
in Toronto offering applied learning and hands-on
experience across various disciplines, preparing
students for real-world careers as well as offering 
them real world experiences. The college emphasizes hands-on, 
practical learning that prepares students for careers in 
industries like technology, health sciences, business, and design.
George Brown College partners with leading companies in Toronto to 
give students valuable real-world experience and co-op opportunities.
GBC’s downtown Toronto campuses — St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront 
— give students access to real-world experiences through partnerships with 
local companies and organizations.
