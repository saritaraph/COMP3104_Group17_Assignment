    Why DeveOps is important in SDLC:
Learning DevOps is essential in the Software 
Development Life Cycle because it bridges the gap 
between development and operations, improving speed, 
reliability, and collaboration as well let deveopers 
have a safey net in case of any issues.
DevOps plays a critical role in the SDLC by 
bridging the gap between developers and operations teams. 
It ensures that code is built, tested, and deployed 
quickly and consistently.
