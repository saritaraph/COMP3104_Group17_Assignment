## Devops Software Development Life Cycle(SDLC)

The importance lies between development and operations, helping teams deliver software faster, more reliably, and with higher quality.

DevOps is all about teamwork between the developers who build the software and the operations team who run it. It helps them work together to make software faster, more reliable, and better quality.

DevOps makes the whole process smoother from planning to delivering the final product. It saves time, reduces mistakes, and helps teams release updates more often.
